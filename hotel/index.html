Kuhns Landing

A strip of land, two lakes, one very old building.

The Barbee chain runs seven lakes deep through Kosciusko County, and this hotel sits on the thin neck of ground where Big Barbee nearly touches Little Barbee. People have been coming out here for dinner since before the roads were paved, when the lakes were the reason you made the trip at all.

The building you walk into went up in 1923, on the footprint of the Ormond Hotel that burned on an August night in 1921. The stone walls, the pressed tin ceiling and the long bar are the originals. So, depending on who you ask, are a few of the guests. The guest rooms are long closed, but the bar, the dining room and the stories all stayed.

Two kitchens, two rooms, two completely different nights. White plates and a prime rib down here. Pizza, a ballgame and the deck upstairs at Danny's.

1897 to now

Room 301 & the Long Story

Lake country was a destination long before the interstate, and this building has been standing in the middle of it the whole time.

  1. 1897

    The Ormond opens at Kuhns Landing

    A hotel goes up on the narrow neck of land between Big and Little Barbee, built for summer people coming out to the lakes.

  2. 1921

    Fire, 17 August

    The Ormond burns to the ground.

  3. 1923

    The New Barbee Hotel

    Clara A. Davison and Charles H. Lincoln rebuild on the same footprint and open in the spring. That is the building you walk into today.

  4. 1920s

    Gable and Lombard

    The hotel becomes a quiet place for Hollywood to be somewhere else. The largest suite upstairs was named for them.

  5. 1930s

    Room 301

    Local legend puts Al Capone, John Dillinger and Baby Face Nelson out here during the Chicago years. The story goes that Capone always took Room 301 for its escape route, and that he would book the whole hotel so there was nobody else in it. People still say they smell cigar smoke under that door.

  6. 2006

    Dan Signore

    The hotel changes hands again and the kitchen is rebuilt around steaks, prime rib and fresh fish.

  7. 2008

    Danny's opens

    The sports bar and pizzeria opens upstairs, giving the Barbee a second kitchen and a second, looser room.

  8. 2014

    On television

    Travel Channel's The Dead Files films at the hotel. Guests had been telling stories about the upstairs hallway for a long time before that.

The Capone, Dillinger and Nelson accounts are local lore passed down with the building rather than documented hotel records. We tell them the way they were told to us. The 1897 opening, the 1921 fire and the 1923 rebuild are a matter of record.
